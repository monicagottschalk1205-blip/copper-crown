What’s on
your roof

Keep scrolling and watch one go together.

  1. The deck

    Solid boards over the rafters. Anything soft or rotted gets swapped before a single layer goes on top.

  2. Ice and water shield

    A sticky membrane along the eaves, right where Colorado snow likes to sit and melt.

  3. Underlayment

    A synthetic layer across the whole roof that keeps shedding water if a shingle ever lifts.

  4. Shingles

    Impact-rated shingles in straight courses, nailed on the nail line and not above it.

  5. Copper ridge and flashing

    Ridge caps, gutters and chimney flashing. The part that makes it a Copper Crown roof.

What we do

Houses, garages, porches and the metalwork in between.

  • Tear-off to ridge cap, usually in a day or two. Tarps over the landscaping and a magnet sweep after.

  • Lifted tabs, bruised shingles and punctured vents, patched fast after the storm rolls through.

  • Standing seam panels for roofs you only want to do once. Quiet, clean lines, sheds snow.

  • Hand-bent flashing, valleys, bay window roofs and gutters that move water away from the house.

  • We walk it, photograph it and tell you plainly: repair it, replace it, or leave it alone.

  • We document the damage and meet your adjuster on the roof so nothing gets missed.
